Extraocular Muscles

Right eye • Anterior view • Nose on your right

In “All muscles in sequence,” the animation advances to the next muscle after each cycle. In “Repeat selected muscle,” the current muscle repeats until you pause or select another muscle. “Next muscle” changes the selected muscle in either playback mode.

Schematic movements are exaggerated for teaching. In the actions mode, each muscle's components are illustrated together; amplitudes do not represent measured force or rotation angles. Clinical testing positions emphasize a muscle's vertical action rather than isolating it completely. Normal gaze requires coordinated activity of multiple muscles.
